SportScore attribution

Powered by SportScore. The scoreboard overlays also show this attribution.

Scores are checked at your chosen interval. The source caches data for around 60 seconds. A score change triggers a goal alert, but scorer names and red cards are NOT automatically supplied in this build. Score decreases trigger a correction alert. Missing fixtures are not invented.

Before Twitch use: ask SportScore to confirm that a visible attribution in a video overlay satisfies its backlink requirement and that the data may be rebroadcast on Twitch. The site describes an HTML dofollow link requirement which video cannot satisfy on its own.

Large fixture days may be incomplete because the published endpoint limits results to 200 and documents no pagination. Use the diagnostics above to check. Do not run replay and live at the same time.
